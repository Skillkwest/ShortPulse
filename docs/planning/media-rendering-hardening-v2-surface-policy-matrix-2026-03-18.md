# Media Rendering Hardening v2 Surface Policy Matrix (2026-03-18)

Last updated: 2026-03-18
Status: Active

## Purpose
Provide one place to decide and lock image-delivery policy per surface before implementation slices change behavior.

## Decision Fields
Each surface row must define:
1. `surface`
2. `primary files`
3. `source classes`
4. `current renderer behavior`
5. `current optimizer behavior`
6. `current signing behavior`
7. `target delivery policy`
8. `fallback order`
9. `proof fields`
10. `status`

## Policy Decision Rules
1. P1 must explicitly choose one target delivery policy per surface.
2. No target delivery policy can be marked `Accepted` until the decision log is updated.
3. If the accepted policy changes durable architecture, add an ADR in the same slice.
4. Policy rows may remain `Pending` during planning, but implementation slices may not proceed on a `Pending` surface.
5. Long-tail surfaces must be represented per surface; bucket rows are not sufficient for `P8` closeout.
