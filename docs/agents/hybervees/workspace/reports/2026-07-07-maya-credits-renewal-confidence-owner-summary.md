# Maya Credits Renewal Confidence: Owner Summary

## Short Version

Maya understands her balance and renewal now. She saw `342 / 350`, Starter, `$15/month`, next renewal `Aug 4, 2026`, and incoming `+350`.

The weak spot is credit usage history. She could not find the two 4-credit image debits, and the Credits page says there are no recent billing events.

## What This Means

The app feels safer for basic billing trust, but not safe enough for heavier credit spending.

If creators cannot audit what used their credits, they will hesitate to buy top-ups, upgrade, or keep spending confidently.

## Do This

Add a customer-visible credit usage ledger on the Credits page, or a clear link to one. It should show recent generation debits with date, workflow/model or media context, and credit amount, while keeping subscription payments and top-up purchases separate.
