## What I Tried

I started from the ShortPulse homepage and clicked Launch App. It sent me to sign-up, so I switched to Sign in and logged in. I landed in AI Studio with `346 / 350` credits visible.

My first instinct was to open Media. That worked: I saw one saved image, and the image looked like the calm small-apartment reset visual from the prior run. That made me feel relieved because at least the paid image was not lost.

Then I opened Projects. This part got a little confusing. The project modal showed `5-minute renter reset tests`, but after I clicked it, the visible card looked mostly like `Open ->` next to `Untitled Project`. I was not sure whether I had selected the right project until I clicked the Open card.

Once the project opened, the top of AI Studio clearly said `5-minute renter reset tests`, and the Reference Grid showed `Media: 1/500` with the image visible. I left to Dashboard and came back through Open Projects, and the image was still there.

I also dragged the image into Quick Slot Inventory. That worked visually, and it felt like I could reuse the image as a reference.
