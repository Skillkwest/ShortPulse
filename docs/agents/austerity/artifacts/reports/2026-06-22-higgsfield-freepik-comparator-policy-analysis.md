# Higgsfield And Freepik/Magnific Comparator Policy Analysis

Date: 2026-06-22

Purpose: summarize how two relevant AI creative platforms position terms, privacy, refunds, commercial use, consent, training, moderation, and legal protection so Austerity can benchmark ShortPulse without copying competitor text.

Authority: comparator research artifact. It is not endorsement of competitor policies and not counsel signoff.

## Comparator Summary

### Higgsfield

Primary sources:

- Terms: https://higgsfield.ai/terms-of-use-agreement
- Privacy: https://higgsfield.ai/privacy-policy
- Trust page: https://higgsfield.ai/trust

Observed posture:

- Requires account registration for some features and makes users responsible for account activity and restricting use by minors.
- Does not claim ownership of user inputs/outputs and says it does not restrict commercial use of outputs.
- Takes a broad license to user content and states inputs and outputs may be used to train, develop, enhance, evolve, and improve AI models and services, and for marketing/promotional purposes.
- Refunds: generally non-refundable; initial purchase refund within 7 days if no credits used; renewals excluded; possible service fee up to 6% where allowed.
- Credits: no cash value, non-transferable, non-reloadable, non-redeemable except as required by law, and may be forfeited on cancellation/cessation.
- Subscriptions auto-renew until canceled; cancellation via account payment settings or support email; access continues to end of term; no prorated refunds.
- Provides IP/takedown contact information and repeat-infringer-style policy.
- Privacy policy discloses public/shared content risk, cookies, Do Not Track non-response, retention after deletion/account closure, international transfer, and under-18 posture.
- Trust page emphasizes Stripe, fraud prevention, content filtering, community moderation, public reporting, user responsibility for uploaded content and likenesses, and legal review for marketing materials.

Lessons for ShortPulse:

- Higgsfield is more aggressive than ShortPulse should be on content/model-training licenses. ShortPulse's no-training-on-private-workspace-content posture is more privacy-protective and must be preserved accurately.
- Higgsfield's refund posture is stricter than ShortPulse's current policy in some ways and gives a clear 7-day/no-credit-use initial-purchase path. ShortPulse can remain discretionary/non-refundable but should keep checkout disclosures plain.
- Higgsfield explicitly handles community/public content. ShortPulse should avoid implying similar community readiness until public UGC controls exist.

### Freepik/Magnific

Primary sources:

- Terms: https://www.magnific.com/nl/legal/terms-of-use
- AI content/copyright docs: https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/ai-content-and-copyright
- Subscriptions/refunds docs: https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/subscriptions-and-refunds
- Plans/pricing docs: https://www.magnific.com/cn/ai/docs/plans-and-pricing

Observed posture:

- Operated by Freepik Company, S.L. from Spain; Spanish law and Malaga courts with EU consumer ODR disclosure.
- Acceptable Use Policy is incorporated into terms.
- AI docs say AI-generated images/videos may be used for personal/professional purposes, but copyright status is evolving globally and ownership cannot be guaranteed in all jurisdictions.
- Paid plans include commercial license for AI-generated content; free plans are personal use/attribution-limited.
- AI-generated content can be used as a central commercial element, unlike some stock-content main/secondary-element restrictions.
- Users remain responsible for third-party IP rights, recognizable likeness consent, copyrighted characters/artworks/brands, AI guidelines, and jurisdiction-specific legality.
- AI-generated content is excluded from standard stock-content legal protection/indemnification; enterprise contracts may differ.
- Refunds: 30-day refund for subscription payments only if no service use occurred: no downloads and no AI credits consumed; current billing period only; no partial annual unused-month refunds after window.
- Extra credit refunds: 14 days if no credits from pack were used.
- Credits: plan credits expire at billing cycle end; extra credits remain valid/frozen under specified conditions.
- Plans distinguish free/personal/attribution from paid/commercial/no-attribution tiers.

Lessons for ShortPulse:

- Freepik/Magnific has a strong plain-English AI commercial-rights pattern: "commercial use allowed, but copyright varies and user bears third-party-rights responsibility." ShortPulse should keep this style.
- ShortPulse should not offer stock-content-style indemnity for AI outputs unless counsel, insurance, and enterprise contracts support it.
- Refund policy can be strict if disclosed: Freepik/Magnific ties eligibility to zero use. ShortPulse's policy similarly should treat used credits/completed generations as generally not refundable.
- If ShortPulse later offers "unlimited" generation, it needs a fair-use/priority-use policy and precise model exceptions like Freepik/Magnific docs use.

## Comparator-To-ShortPulse Decisions

| Topic | Higgsfield | Freepik/Magnific | ShortPulse recommended posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Output ownership | Does not claim ownership; allows commercial use | Commercial use for paid AI plans; ownership/copyright not guaranteed | Allow conditional commercial use; do not guarantee copyrightability or non-infringement |
| Model training | Broad training/improvement/marketing license over inputs and outputs | Not the key surfaced docs reviewed here | Preserve no-training on private workspace content unless explicitly changed |
| Public/community content | Has public/shared content and moderation language | Stock/content platform with acceptable use | ShortPulse private by default; public UGC is a separate gate |
| Refunds | 7-day initial purchase if no credits used; renewals excluded | 30-day subscription refund if zero use; 14-day unused credit-pack refund | Non-refundable by default, discretionary/required-by-law/recognized-failure review; disclose before checkout |
| Credits | No cash value; may be forfeited | Plan credits expire; extra credits/frozen rules | Credits not cash/stored value; profile says credits do not expire, so do not copy expiration language |
| Likeness consent | User responsibility and media upload agreement | User responsible for recognizable likeness consent | Keep media-rights gate; add workflow-specific consent for voice/likeness expansion |
| Legal protection | Disclaimers/liability cap | AI output excluded from standard indemnity | No indemnity for AI outputs without enterprise/counsel-approved contract |
| Governing law/disputes | Arbitration/class waiver with opt-out | Spanish law/courts, EU ODR | Counsel review required before adding arbitration/class waiver or changing Arizona venue |

## ShortPulse Should Not Copy

- Higgsfield's broad perpetual model-training/marketing license unless the user intentionally changes ShortPulse's privacy/product posture.
- Freepik/Magnific's stock-content indemnity structure, because ShortPulse does not operate a licensed stock marketplace and should not guarantee AI-output legality.
- Competitor refund windows without matching checkout, support, and billing implementation.
- Competitor "unlimited" language without fair-use limits and exact model exceptions.

## ShortPulse Should Adopt As Pattern

- Plain-English commercial-use section.
- Explicit "copyright varies by jurisdiction" caution.
- User responsibility for third-party IP, recognizable likenesses, brands, characters, and platform/legal disclosure.
- No AI-output legal protection/indemnity unless enterprise contract says otherwise.
- Simple support/billing/refund request path from the account email.
- Public trust/safety posture that matches actual moderation and support capacity.
