## One-Sentence Legal Posture

ShortPulse should be handled as an adult-only, authenticated, private creative workspace for user-directed AI media generation, not as a public UGC platform, youth service, biometric identity service, social network, AI replica marketplace, or guaranteed-output commercial-rights provider.

## Durable Operating Rules

1. Private generation is lower risk than public hosting. Public sharing, galleries, profiles, community, collaboration, or public hosted UGC create a separate legal launch gate.
2. AI output rights are conditional. Do not promise uniqueness, copyrightability, non-infringement, platform acceptance, commercial fitness, or unrestricted ownership.
3. User rights and consent must come before generation. Users must represent they have rights to prompts, uploads, media, likenesses, voices, brands, client material, and any third-party content they provide.
4. Voice, face, likeness, identity, celebrity/public-figure, and minor-related workflows are high risk by default. Use explicit consent/legal-right gates and default prohibitions for deceptive, sexualized, exploitative, or unauthorized uses.
5. CSAM, child exploitation, grooming, sexualized minors, NCII, sexual deepfakes, nudification, and intimate-image abuse are zero-tolerance categories.
6. Section 230 is not the plan. It may help for some third-party content claims, but it does not cover IP claims, federal criminal law, electronic privacy claims, or content ShortPulse materially develops.
7. DMCA hygiene is needed before public hosting and high ROI even for private storage: copyright contact, agent designation if seeking safe harbor, takedown/counter-notice process, repeat-infringer policy, and evidence records.
8. Provider terms flow through to users. ShortPulse policies must require lawful use and compliance with OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Fal, Kie/other model providers, and destination-platform rules.
9. Privacy disclosures must name the actual AI workflow: prompts, uploads, generated outputs, voice samples, face/likeness material, billing, telemetry, provider processing, retention, deletion, rights, and sale/share posture.
10. International launch is not passive. EU/UK/Canada/Australia exposure requires fresh privacy, AI, consumer, hosting, and safety review; mainland China should be excluded unless there is dedicated China counsel and a compliance plan.

## Policy Stack ShortPulse Needs

Minimum public policy stack before launch:

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy
- Refund Policy
- Acceptable Use Policy, either standalone or embedded in Terms
- Copyright/DMCA contact and takedown process
- Abuse/reporting paths for copyright, privacy, impersonation/likeness, NCII/intimate-image abuse, child safety, and billing/refunds

Do not publish final policy pages from this synthesis alone. It is a reference layer for drafting and review.

## Terms Of Service Concepts To Preserve

Terms should cover:

- eligibility, account responsibility, and authority to bind a business/client;
- user content license needed to operate ShortPulse;
- user warranties for rights, consents, permissions, and lawful use;
- upload, prompt, media, likeness, voice, brand, client-material, and third-party rights responsibilities;
- AI output caveats: no guarantee of uniqueness, copyrightability, non-infringement, platform acceptance, commercial performance, or legal fitness;
- provider terms and destination-platform compliance;
- acceptable-use prohibitions;
- copyright/takedown process and repeat-infringer policy;
- suspension/termination for abuse or legal risk;
- indemnity, warranty disclaimers, liability limits, governing law, venue, arbitration/class-waiver posture if approved by counsel.

## Privacy Policy Concepts To Preserve

Privacy policy should explain:

- categories collected: account, billing, prompts, uploads, generated outputs, voice samples, media metadata, support messages, device/usage/telemetry, cookies/local storage;
- purposes: account operation, generation, storage, billing, support, security, abuse prevention, analytics, legal compliance;
- AI/provider processing and subprocessors;
- billing processor handling;
- retention and deletion;
- privacy rights and request process;
- sensitive data and biometric caveats;
- children/minors posture;
- sale/share/targeted advertising posture;
- international transfers;
- security practices at a truthful, non-overpromising level.

Do not claim ShortPulse does not process biometric data unless Dave/engineering proof confirms that no voiceprint, face geometry, biometric template, or identification artifact is created, retained, or inferred.

## Refund Policy Concepts To Preserve

Refund policy should align with actual Stripe, credit, storage, and generation behavior:

- subscriptions renew unless cancelled;
- cancellation timing and downgrade timing must match product behavior;
- credits have no cash value unless law requires otherwise;
- used credits are generally not refundable;
- generation failures should release or restore credits where records support failure;
- aesthetic dissatisfaction, prompt mistakes, platform rejection, user-upload problems, abandoned flows, or consumed generation work should not create automatic refunds unless approved;
- taxes, currency, payment processor fees, chargebacks, and legal exceptions must be addressed;
- EU/UK/state consumer rights may require jurisdiction-specific exceptions.

## Acceptable Use Baseline

ShortPulse should prohibit:

- illegal content or illegal activity;
- CSAM, grooming, child exploitation, and sexualized minors, including AI-generated content;
- NCII, sexual deepfakes, nudification, unauthorized sexualized likenesses, and intimate-image abuse;
- unauthorized voice, face, likeness, identity, celebrity, public-figure, private-person, government, or business impersonation;
- scams, phishing, fraud, fake endorsements, fake testimonials, deceptive ads, and synthetic-person deception;
- robocalls, robotexts, voter suppression, political impersonation, synthetic campaign deception, and public-interest deception without required consent, authority, and disclosures;
- harassment, threats, hate, extortion, blackmail, and abusive targeting;
- copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, contract, confidentiality, and trade-secret violations;
- malware, credential theft, scraping, circumvention, rate-limit abuse, and service interference;
- regulated professional advice presented as final professional advice without qualified review;
- provider/platform policy evasion.

## Feature Gates

| Custom voice cloning                      | High risk                 | affirmative consent/legal right, ownership ledger, no deception/sexualization, provider compliance       |
| Realistic face/likeness generation        | High risk                 | consent/right warranties, publicity/privacy warnings, no impersonation/deceptive/adult misuse            |

| Adult/sexual generation                   | Very high risk            | default prohibit until age assurance, consent tooling, moderation, provider approval, and counsel review |
| Public gallery/community/profile sharing  | Separate launch gate      | DMCA, NCII/TIDA, CSAM/NCMEC, moderation, reporting, appeals, DSA/UK OSA review                           |
| Export to social/ad platforms             | Allow with caveats        | AI/synthetic-media disclosure reminder and platform-policy compliance                                    |

| Mainland China availability               | Block by default          | dedicated China counsel, localization, content controls, labeling, filing/security assessment analysis   |

## Public Sharing Gate

Do not treat public sharing as a small UI feature. If ShortPulse hosts or distributes user content publicly, add:

- DMCA designated-agent/contact/process and repeat-infringer handling;
- copyright takedown/counter-notice records;
- NCII/TIDA intake, removal timing, and evidence-preservation procedure;
- CSAM/NCMEC escalation SOP with minimal-handling controls;
- impersonation/likeness/privacy abuse intake;
- moderation rules, account enforcement, notice, appeal/review path, and repeat-abuse policy;
- EU DSA and UK OSA applicability review;
- abuse-report UI and internal owner workflow;
- logs for takedown, appeal, enforcement, and restoration decisions.

## Evidence To Preserve

Preserve these records wherever product design makes them available:

- media-rights agreement version and acceptance timestamp;
- voice consent/ownership records;
- generation request IDs and provider task IDs;
- credit reservation, capture, refund, and release records;
- upload ownership, storage path, deletion, and retention events;
- takedown requests, counter-notices, appeals, action timestamps, and repeat-offender decisions;
- privacy request intake and response records;
- refund request, Stripe invoice/payment intent, credit ledger, and decision records;
- safety escalation records, with minimized access to harmful content.

## Future Research Refresh Triggers

Refresh sources before:

- publishing or materially changing Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy, or AUP;

- allowing youth access or marketing to minors;

- changing provider stack or destination-platform export support.

## Counsel Questions To Keep Open

- Final legal entity, address, legal email, DMCA agent, governing law, venue, arbitration, and class-waiver posture.
- Whether launch is U.S.-only or actively international.
- Whether EU/UK users are intentionally served or marketed to.
- Whether any current or planned voice/face workflow creates biometric identifiers, voiceprints, face geometry, or identity artifacts.
- Whether ShortPulse will use targeted ads, retargeting, lookalike audiences, pixels, or sale/share of personal information.
- Exact subscription, credit, refund, cancellation, storage add-on, and EU/UK withdrawal posture.
- Whether any public sharing feature is on the near-term roadmap.
- Whether adult/sexual, public-figure, parody/news, political, or celebrity/brand likeness workflows are allowed, prohibited, or counsel-reviewed exceptions.
