# Bopper Reports

Purpose: hold fuller average-user workflow and trust-friction audits.

## Conventions

- Keep one detailed report per meaningful checkpoint or route bundle.
- Prefer dense, high-signal writing over long narrative.
- Treat this as part of the real operator record, not the lightweight trainer summary.
- Always separate:
  - what Bopper clicked
  - what Bopper expected
  - what actually happened
  - what caused confusion or abandonment
- Also preserve the technical/operator detail needed to make changes:
  - route and environment specifics
  - runtime/evidence references
  - code/doc follow-up surfaces
  - likely ownership or first inspection points
