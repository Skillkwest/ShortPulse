# ShortPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortPulse collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you use our websites, applications, AI Studio tools, media library, billing features, profile features, support channels, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

"ShortPulse," "we," "us," and "our" mean Arc Creative Enterprises LLC, doing business as ShortPulse, the operator of the Service.

This Privacy Policy applies to ShortPulse's processing of personal information. It does not replace our Terms of Service, Refund Policy, media-rights requirements, provider terms, or any separate agreement that expressly applies to a specific feature or business relationship.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, do not use the Service.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, information generated through your use of ShortPulse, information from service providers, and information collected automatically.

### Account Information

We may collect account identifiers and profile information, including your email address, user ID, authentication status, profile settings, workspace settings, subscription status, account events, and related account metadata.

Authentication may be provided by Supabase or another authentication provider. ShortPulse does not need your password in plain text.

### Billing Information

If you subscribe, buy credits, buy storage add-ons, change plans, cancel, or use paid features, we may collect billing-related information, including Stripe customer IDs, subscription IDs, checkout session IDs, invoice IDs, payment status, plan names, billing interval, credit grants, credit balances, refunds, disputes, tax information, and transaction metadata.

Payment-card and bank-account details are processed by Stripe or another payment processor. ShortPulse does not intend to store full payment-card numbers.

### Creative Workspace And User Content

We collect and process the content you submit, upload, generate, save, organize, or otherwise use in ShortPulse, including prompts, text, images, videos, audio, voice samples, likeness materials, product references, style references, project data, workspace preferences, media-library items, generated outputs, draft assets, issue-report messages, and related metadata.

This content may include personal information about you or other people if you upload or generate content containing names, faces, voices, likenesses, locations, identifiers, or other identifiable details.

Because ShortPulse supports media and voice workflows, Your Content may include sensitive information or information that some laws treat specially, such as face images, voice recordings, likeness material, identity material, biometric-related information, or information about minors. You are responsible for deciding whether that content is appropriate to submit and for obtaining any required rights and consents.

### Generation And Provider Data

When you use AI features, we may collect prompts, inputs, settings, model choices, provider names, job identifiers, task status, output URLs, error codes, reservation and credit events, generation costs, timing metadata, and other operational information needed to submit, track, deliver, troubleshoot, bill, and audit generation requests.

### Support, Feedback, And Reports

If you contact us or submit an issue report, we may collect your message, authenticated user context, email address, source path, screenshots or attachments you provide, browser or device information, and related support metadata.

### Device, Usage, Telemetry, And Log Information

We may collect technical and usage information, including IP address or approximate network information, browser type, device type, operating system, pages and routes viewed, referrer host, landing path, request identifiers, user-agent strings, hosting metadata, timestamps, error metadata, event names, feature usage, performance metrics, storage usage, and security or audit logs.

We may create aggregated, deidentified, or statistical information from Service usage, billing, generation, telemetry, and support data. We may use that information to operate, measure, secure, and improve ShortPulse, provided it is not used to identify you.

### Attribution And Growth Telemetry

ShortPulse may collect attribution and growth telemetry, including anonymous browser identifiers stored in localStorage, UTM source/medium/campaign values, landing paths, referrer hosts, page-view events, CTA events, pricing interactions, signup events, checkout events, and authenticated-user stitching when you sign in.

### Cookies, Local Storage, Session Storage, And Similar Technologies

ShortPulse may use browser localStorage, sessionStorage, cookies, tokens, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember workspace preferences, preserve project state, store anonymous attribution identifiers, support checkout handoff, report client errors, and operate the Service.

ShortPulse currently treats cookies and similar technologies as operational, authentication, security, billing, workspace-preference, product-telemetry, and first-party attribution tools. ShortPulse is not currently designed to use third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, lookalike advertising audiences, or cross-context behavioral advertising. If that changes, we will update this policy and provide any legally required notice, consent, cookie controls, opt-out links, or global privacy control handling before using those tools.

## 3. AI Providers, Training, And Product Improvement

ShortPulse uses third-party AI and infrastructure providers to perform actions you request. Your prompts, uploads, media, voice samples, references, settings, and related metadata may be sent to providers such as OpenAI, Fal, Kie.ai, ElevenLabs, and similar model or infrastructure providers when needed to generate, transform, analyze, store, or deliver requested outputs.

Provider processing may be governed by provider terms, privacy policies, acceptable-use policies, safety systems, retention practices, data-processing terms, and technical limits. ShortPulse does not control every provider system, model behavior, safety decision, outage, or retention practice.

ShortPulse does not currently use your private workspace content to train ShortPulse-owned foundation models. We also do not intentionally opt in to third-party AI provider model training on your private workspace content when provider settings or account terms give us that choice.

ShortPulse may still use information to improve the product without training a foundation model. For example, we may use logs, task status, cost data, error records, support reports, safety events, feature usage, aggregated metrics, and deidentified statistics to debug features, reduce failed jobs, improve billing accuracy, detect abuse, measure performance, and decide what to build next.

You should not upload or submit sensitive personal information, confidential business information, regulated information, or third-party personal information unless you have the rights and authority to do so and the use is appropriate for an AI generation workflow.

ShortPulse is not designed for protected health information, financial-account numbers, government identifiers, children's data, trade secrets you are not authorized to process, or other highly regulated information unless we expressly agree to that use in writing.
