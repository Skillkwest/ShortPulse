# Product Quality Ladders

Purpose: define the shared ladders and labels used to rate ShortPulse itself.

## Route Quality Ladder

- `9.0 - 10.0`: polished and confidence-building
- `7.0 - 8.9`: usable with friction
- `5.0 - 6.9`: weak, confusing, or inconsistent
- `below 5.0`: trust-breaking or workflow-threatening

## Product Confidence Rule

- `high`: route and workflow truth are backed by direct evidence and the lane was realistic
- `medium`: product truth is usable, but some part of the lane or score remains judgmental
- `low`: evidence or realism is too weak for stable product scoring

## Workflow Completion Ladder

- `validated`: believable user can start and complete the workflow
- `partial`: meaningful parts work, but completion truth is incomplete
- `blocked`: believable path fails or dead-ends
- `not-started`: not yet tested

## Issue Severity Ladder

- `P0`: product path unusable or severe regression
- `P1`: major trust break or core workflow failure
- `P2`: meaningful friction, confusion, or partial failure
- `P3`: polish or lower-severity friction

## Confidence Ladder

- `high`: direct evidence is strong and route behavior is clear
- `medium`: evidence is usable but one part remains somewhat inferential
- `low`: evidence is partial or the route behavior is not yet stable
