## What I Tried

I signed in through the normal email path. I accidentally hit the Google path first, which felt like a very normal customer mistake because the sign-in page has several sign-in choices. I backed out and signed in with email.

On the dashboard, I saw `342 / 350` credits and Starter plan. That made me feel safe enough to keep exploring because I knew I was not starting a credit-spending action.

I opened `Open Projects`, found `5-minute renter reset tests`, and clicked the project. The first click seemed to select the project and show `Open`; clicking the project tile again opened AI Studio. That was not a big problem, but I did have a small "did I do that right?" moment.

In AI Studio, I saw:

- project name: `5-minute renter reset tests`
- credits: `342 / 350`
- `Quick Slot Inventory`
- `Reference Grid`
- `Media: 2/500`
- two image thumbnails in the right rail
- `Generate` showing `4`

That was reassuring and confusing at the same time. I was happy the two images came back. I was not sure if Reference Grid was a saved library, a temporary tray, or the thing that tells generation what images to use.

I clicked `Media` because I wanted to compare it to Reference Grid. Media showed `2 SAVED`, and the same two images were there. That helped. It made me believe the images are truly saved somewhere.

Then I clicked one saved image in Media. It selected the image and showed `Delete from library`. That made me pause. I expected something like `Use as reference`, `Add to Reference Grid`, or `View details`. Instead the most obvious new action was destructive, so I did not want to keep clicking.

I also clicked the top `Reference Grid` label. I expected it to focus or explain Reference Grid, but it hid the grid. Clicking it again brought the grid back. Later I clicked `Quick Slot Inventory`, and it hid that section too. Once I saw it happen, I understood these are show/hide controls, but I would not have guessed that from the labels.

I reopened the project once more and saw a restore checklist: verify session, check media agreement, resolve project, load workspace, prepare studio. I actually liked that. It made me feel like the app was restoring a real saved workspace, and the two images came back.

## What Felt Clear

- My project still exists.
- My balance is still `342 / 350`.
- The two generated images are still saved in Media.
- The same two images show in Reference Grid.
- Clicking `Reference Grid` and `Quick Slot Inventory` can hide/show those right-rail sections.
- AI Studio restore explains what the app is doing before the workspace loads.

## What Felt Unclear

- I do not know whether Reference Grid is a saved library, an active prompt reference area, or a temporary visual tray.
- I do not know if images in Reference Grid automatically affect the next generation.
- I do not know whether selecting a Media item should help me reuse it or only manage/delete it.
- The `Reference Grid` and `Quick Slot Inventory` top labels look like navigation, but they behave like visibility toggles.
- The icon actions on reference thumbnails are not plain enough for me to trust them without hovering or guessing.
- I would be nervous clicking anything that looks like remove/delete because I do not want to lose paid images.

## Product Decision Signal

- `Trust`: Saved-work trust improved because the images persisted and restored. Reuse-workflow trust is still weak.
- `Retention`: I would come back, but I would keep using the product cautiously until references are clearer.
- `Support`: I would likely ask support what Reference Grid actually does before spending more credits.
- `Revenue/Credits`: This directly affects spend readiness. I am not ready to pay 4 more credits if I do not know which images are guiding the next generation.
- `Launch readiness`: The underlying saved-work behavior is promising, but first-time explanation of Reference Grid and Quick Slot needs more customer-facing clarity.

Are those images saved there permanently, or are they active references for my next generation? I am afraid to click remove/delete or generate again until I know what is happening.

Bad review / public complaint risk:

```text
ShortPulse saved my images, but the workspace labels are confusing enough that I do not feel safe reusing them without guessing. I should not need to understand a whole system just to reuse an image I already paid for.
```

What would calm me down:

- Plain helper copy that says what Reference Grid is for.
- A visible distinction between saved Media and active generation references.
- Safer wording around remove/delete actions.
- A clear `Use as reference` or `Add to Reference Grid` action from Media.
- Toggle controls that look like toggles or show visible on/off state.

## What I Would Do Next

I would continue cautiously and avoid spending credits for now. My next customer move would be to ask support or wait for clearer UI before generating another image from these references. I like that the images are saved and restored, but I do not feel confident enough to use Reference Grid as part of a real weekly workflow yet.
