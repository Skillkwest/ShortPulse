## What I Tried

I started from the logged-in dashboard in Chrome. I wanted to understand where my projects are, how many credits I have, where AI Studio starts, and where a prompt or generated asset would go before I spent anything.

The dashboard gave me two clear choices: `New Project` and `Open Projects`. I opened projects first because I wanted to see what was already there. I saw a project named `Mayaa chen`, so I opened that instead of making another project.

Inside AI Studio, I looked around the main navigation. I could see `Create`, `Edit`, `Video`, `Sound`, `Media`, `Characters`, `Elements`, `Presets`, `Styles`, `My Generations`, `Canvas`, `Quick Slot Inventory`, and `Reference Grid`. That gave me a basic map, but it was still a lot at once.

I typed a realistic image prompt for my apartment reset routine, checked model options, visited the credits page, came back, and tried saving a text prompt through Reference Grid and Media Library.

## What Felt Clear

- The dashboard was easy to understand at first.
- The credit balance was easy to find on the dashboard, AI Studio, and credits page.
- The model menu helped once I opened it. I could see costs like Seedream 4.5 at 4 credits, Nano Banana 2 at 5 credits, Nano Banana Pro at 8 credits, and GPT Image 2 at 2 credits.
- Reference Grid made more sense after I pinned a text prompt. I could see the prompt appear there and the media count change to `1/500`.

## Where I Got Unsure

- I did not understand what `CREDITS 100 / 0` meant. I understood I had 100 credits, but the `/ 0` part made me pause.
- I expected a simple generate area to tell me the cost before I clicked, but I had to open the model menu to see the model costs.
- `My Generations` sounded like the exact place I would go to find my work later, but it said `COMING SOON`.
- I typed a prompt, went to the credits page, came back, and the prompt was gone. I did not see a warning or a draft restore.
- I clicked `Save to media library` for a pinned text reference. The save button disappeared, but Media still said `No saved items found` and `Loaded 0 media items`.

## What I Figured Out

My basic map is: dashboard starts the work, projects organize the work, AI Studio is where I make things, Reference Grid holds active references, and Media is supposed to be where saved work lives.

I can understand that much. What I still do not understand is the relationship between Reference Grid, Media, and My Generations. If I save a prompt or generate an image, I need the app to make it very obvious where it went.

## What Happened With The Generation

I did not generate anything. This was an orientation run, and I stopped before spending credits because I was not confident enough about saved work yet.

Not confidently.

## How I Felt About It

The app feels powerful, and the dashboard is friendlier than I expected. I can tell there is a serious creator workspace here.

But I felt nervous about losing work. The moment my drafted prompt disappeared after checking credits, I stopped trusting the workflow a little. Then the save-to-media-library check made that worse because I still could not find the saved prompt in Media.

I would not spend credits until I understood the save path better.

## My Honest Take

I would keep exploring ShortPulse because the workspace looks useful and the model cost menu helped me understand pricing. But right now I would not use it for a real campaign without a small test first.

My harsher review version would be: "This looks powerful, but if I cannot trust where my prompts and assets are saved, I would not risk using it during a real content work session."
